# Flashbang

Flashbang is a dating app built for iOS, android, and web. 
It will be made with Expo, hosted with Vercel, will use a convex backend and will use vercels "ai sdk" for an ai powered algorithm.

The primary difference between this and other dating apps will be that, rather than showing you people and having you click either yes or no, you will leave comments about each profile. Those comments will help the AI narrow down what kind of people you're looking for and will suggest more refined picks to your preferences as you keep leaving comments. Comments won't be visible to users, but they will be able to see a summary of general sentiment towards each picture that they put there (and, more specifically, sentiment from people that the algorithm deems them likely to be attracted to).
